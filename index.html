About Stratify

Hi, I'm Brady Nicholson —
the person behind Stratify.

I started Stratify Systems after years of watching small business owners struggle with the same problems I'd seen large organisations spend millions solving — manual processes that eat hours, data scattered across spreadsheets, websites that don't convert, and strategies that sound good on paper but never get implemented.

Small businesses deserve the same quality of thinking, technology, and honest guidance — without the enterprise price tag or a consultant report that gathers dust in a drawer. That belief is what Stratify is built on.

Every project is personal. You'll work with me directly, not a junior team member or an offshore contractor. I'll learn your business, understand your constraints, and build solutions that actually fit — whether that's a custom web application, an automated workflow, or a clear strategic plan that gives your team real direction.

Based in Australia · Available for remote & in-person engagements
